# B1 — verdict: CONFIRMED (re-weighted: the SNIPPET half is the production defect; the PAGE half is latent; the "5/5 Sources" sub-claim is a fixture artifact)

- **Why.** The mechanism is exactly as stated and I could not make it go away: `buildDossier` (`packages/core/src/engine/prompt.ts:158-161`) renders `evidence.slice(0,48)` and `extracted.filter(ok).slice(0,14)` of ONE store shared by every agent (`research-engine.ts:270`, passed whole at `:773-774`/`:782-783`), appended in fetch/search order by `gather.ts:288-292` (sources, dedup by URL — a cached or already-seen URL keeps its EARLY position) and `:351-356` (pages; a cached hit at `:328-341` never re-appends). The loop's own conversation is discarded (`gather` returns `{turns, stop}` only; `runAgent` builds fresh `messages` at `:743` and the writer at `:796` gets only the dossier), so a writer sees nothing its loop read except through the store head. But the finders measured the wrong half. Both REAL July traces (`out/local-4837f6e3`, `out/local-aa4b3edf`) land only 8 and 11 ok pages in the store — under 14 — so **no honest page was ever hidden from any writer in production** (market/competition/compliance/risk/financial/market-refiner fetch 0 pages; B-legit's "modest honest model" fetches every other turn, which today's Gemini does not). What IS binding is `MAX_SNIPPETS=48` against **199 and 174 sources**: my check of every writer's citations (`refute-b1.test.ts`, second describe, verified against the traces) finds 35/39 and 38/52 distinct cited URLs inside the first 48, the rest being fetched `[P]` pages, and **0 and 3 citations across the 151/126 sources past the cut** (the 3 are pages someone fetched). Wave 1 alone runs 29/31 searches (×≤8 Brave results), so every wave-2/3 producer's paid searches (valuation 8, market-refiner 4, financial 1, risk 1 in trace 1 = 14 of 43 searches, ≈$0.22 of $0.88 search spend + loop tokens) return snippets rendered to nobody's writer, and the deal-scout's own marketplace results (beyond-48 hosts in trace 1: bizbuysell 10, bizquest 4, loopnet 4, dealstream 4) never reach the writer that builds the shortlist. Reproduced in the mock at production density (`refute-b1.test.ts` first test, 3 agents, 5 fresh results/query): a wave-2 producer's 15 own results → 0 rendered as `[S]`, its 1 fetched page → rendered; store 75/1. Attacker side: the crowd is real but its incremental damage over "the scout got steered" is small (in July the store head IS the scout's pages anyway, since wave-1 peers fetch nothing; the crowd only displaces the 1-2 pages wave-2 producers fetch), and the "buyer Sources 5/5 attacker" number is the fixture's `boost: 9/10` ranking with `slice(0,5)` (`test/fixtures/fake-web.ts:252-256`) — Sources is UNSLICED (`sources.derive` gets `evidence.sources` whole), so insertion order cannot touch it; that number belongs to M-B3/M-C2, not B1.
- **Severity: P1.** Damage statement: *the flagship writes every wave-2+ section, and the deal-scout's own shortlist, blind to the search results those agents paid for — 48 of ~175-200 sources render, in insertion order, so a producer's own searches reach its own writer only if it ran in the first ~6 searches of the job; the page cap (14) is not reached by today's Gemini runs (8-11 pages) but binds the moment producers fetch as the prompt asks.*
- **Fix: agree with OWN-FIRST, with three sharpenings.** (1) "Touched by this loop" = by URL: every result URL a `web_search` returned to THIS loop (incl. ones `seenUrls` already had — the store entry stays where it is, it is the RENDER that is reordered), every ok `fetch_page` URL, every cached-hit URL; keep it on the per-agent `research` local so a same-dispatch retry that reuses evidence keeps its view. Thread it into BOTH `buildProducerSynthPrompt` and `buildEnricherSynthPrompt` (`prompt.ts:398`, `:432` — the refiners take the enricher path). (2) Add a tier 2 before "the rest": pages/sources whose URL appears in the `current`/context JSON the writer is handed (`sourceUrl` of shortlist/deep_dives) — that is how valuation/financial/deep-dive-refiner get the scout's listing pages WITHOUT re-reading them (in July the refiner re-read 2/4 pages, so cached-hit-as-touched covers it, but not by construction). (3) Per-domain cap only in the FOREIGN tier: an honest scout with 12 bizbuysell listings must keep them all in its own tier; a single domain flooding someone ELSE's tier is the crowd. Hidden honest cost of plain own-first: a writer whose own tier fills the cap loses the market/competition context snippets (valuation-analyst 8 searches × 8 = 64 own → 0 head) — acceptable, its context arrives via `contextBlock`/handoffs, not `[S]`; and in the 45-page mock the refiner sees 1 scout listing instead of 3 (own 5 first) — tier 2 fixes that. "Most recent 14/48": rejected for the finder's reason (cached hits don't re-append; the scout's early listing pages fall out) — verified from the store-push code, not re-run. On 14 being "a number not a property": the number is a proxy for a ~85k-char page block + ~12k-char snippet block; the defect is the missing PRIORITY, not the size — keep a cap (a byte budget spent by tier would waste less on 200-char pages than a count) but do not raise 14 to hide the ordering bug. Checkpoint last-60 vs dossier first-14 (`research-engine.ts:240,353`): only diverges past 60 pages (July 8-11, mock 45); on such a resume the head shifts to pages N-59…N-46 — noted, no damage today, and own-first makes it moot for own pages.
